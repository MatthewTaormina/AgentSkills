## How to refresh this index

1. Open [https://openrouter.ai/providers](https://openrouter.ai/providers).
2. Optionally `GET https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/providers` (Bearer `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`) for slug, HQ, ToS, privacy, status page.
3. `GET https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models` for current model IDs and per-provider prices.
